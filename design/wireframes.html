For you · Migrant Worker

▲ Critical §4.2 · Probation period

Your contract sets a 9-month probation. Dutch law caps this at 2 months for a 1-year contract. Anything longer is NOT enforceable.

What to do

  • Ask for it to be reduced to 2 months in writing.
  • If signed, the cap still applies — you can leave without notice after month 2.

§4.2 Probation

The law (BW 7:652) says probation may not exceed 2 months for a contract of 1 year or longer.

Anything beyond that is void — meaning the clause doesn't apply, even if you signed it.
