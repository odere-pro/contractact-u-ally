# Protected Categories

Special legal protections for specific employee classes and categories.

## Pregnancy and maternity

**Absolute dismissal prohibition**: Cannot dismiss during pregnancy or 16 weeks after childbirth (or end of maternity leave)

- Applies regardless of other "valid" dismissal grounds
- Protections apply retroactively if employer learns of pregnancy post-dismissal

**Maternity leave**: 16 weeks total; can begin 4-6 weeks pre-birth; must include 8 weeks post-birth; full salary (100%) paid by UWV

**Job protection**: Same or equivalent position upon return; cannot be reassigned to inferior role; returning salary cannot be reduced

**Lactation breaks**: Employer must provide reasonable breaks for pumping/breastfeeding (typically 20-30 min, 2× daily, for first 6 months)

## Disability

**Non-discrimination**: Cannot discriminate in hiring, pay, or treatment

**Reasonable accommodation**: Employer must adjust workplace/duties unless undue hardship or fundamental job alteration required

**Dismissal**: Cannot dismiss solely due to disability; if cannot perform essential duties even with accommodation, requires UWV approval; retraining/redeployment must be attempted first

**Wage protection**: Certified disabled employees cannot be paid below minimum wage even if productivity lower

## Trade union activity

**Protected rights**:

- Joining union without discrimination
- Protected time for union representation
- Collective bargaining (employer must negotiate in good faith)
- Lawful strike participation

**Union representative protection**: Special protections for works council members; dismissal triggers heightened court scrutiny

**Works council (Ondernemingsraad)**: Employees in orgs with 50+ can establish council with consultation rights on major business decisions

## Whistleblower protection

**Protected disclosures**: Cannot be dismissed for reporting illegal activities (internal or external)

**Scope**: Financial fraud, environmental violations, health/safety breaches, discrimination, labour law violations

**Procedure**: Good faith reporting (genuine concern, not malicious); should follow internal process if available; can report to authorities if internal fails

**Burden of proof**: If dismissed after reporting violation, dismissal presumed retaliatory; employer must prove legitimate independent reason

## Age discrimination

**Employment**: Cannot refuse to hire or dismiss due to age (rare exceptions for law enforcement/military)

**Compensation**: Cannot pay less due to age (apprentice rates for young workers permitted; cannot reduce at retirement age)

**Seniority**: Seniority-based pay increases permitted if applied objectively (not disguised age discrimination)
