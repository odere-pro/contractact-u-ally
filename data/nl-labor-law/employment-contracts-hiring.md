# Employment Contracts & Hiring

Formation, types, and mandatory elements of employment contracts in the Netherlands.

## Key concepts

- **Written vs. Verbal**: Contracts not legally required in writing, but employer must provide written information within 1 week
- **Mandatory Information**: Names, addresses, start date, salary, hours, holiday entitlement, notice periods, applicable CAO
- **Contract Types**: Permanent (vaste aanstellingen), fixed-term (bepaalde tijd), temporary agency, zero-hour, internship, part-time
- **Automatic Conversion**: Fixed-term contracts automatically become permanent after 3 renewals OR 36 months total duration
- **Probation Period**: Maximum 2 months (extendable to 3 months by written agreement); immediate termination allowed without cause during probation
- **False Self-Employment**: Workers treated as employees if lack control over work, cannot refuse assignments, or work exclusively for one client

## Critical rules

- **Late Information**: If employer fails to provide information within 1 month, employee can demand it; failure exposes employer to court action
- **Ambiguous Terms**: Interpreted contra proferentem (against drafting party—usually employer)
- **Probation Trap**: Cannot extend probation after initial period ends or use successive contracts to reset probation
- **Fixed-Term Limits**: Cannot create brief breaks between contracts to "reset" the 36-month counter—courts reject this practice
