# Dutch Labour Law

Comprehensive reference guide for employment law in the Netherlands, covering employment contracts, termination procedures, working conditions, compensation, and dispute resolution.

## Overview

dutch-labour

Dutch labour law is among the most protective in Europe. Key principles:

- **Fundamental prohibition on unilateral termination**: Employer cannot terminate without UWV approval, court order, or employee written consent
- **Strong employee protections**: Minimum notice periods, severance requirements, protected dismissal grounds
- **Extensive leave entitlements**: 4× working days annual leave minimum, statutory maternity leave (16 weeks), parental leave options
- **Stringent wage protections**: Minimum wage indexed annually, 8% holiday allowance, equal pay requirements
- **Special protections**: Pregnancy, disability, union activity, whistleblowing

## Legal authorities

- **Ministry of Social Affairs and Employment (SZW)**: Policy and enforcement
- **UWV (Employee Insurance Agency)**: Dismissal approvals, disability benefits
- **Labour Inspectorate (Inspectie SZW)**: Workplace compliance
- **District Courts (Rechtbank)**: Employment disputes
- **Collective Labour Agreements (CAO)**: Sector-specific terms that override statutory minimums
