# Dispute Resolution & Enforcement

Court procedures, available remedies, and enforcement mechanisms for employment law violations.

## Court system

**Cantonal Court (Kantongerecht)**:

- Small claims up to €25,000
- Employment disputes, wage claims
- Faster, less formal process

**District Court (Rechtbank)**:

- Larger employment claims
- Wrongful dismissal (must file within 1 year of dismissal)
- Can request interim relief (reinstatement pending trial)

**Court of Appeals (Gerechtshof)**: Appeals from lower courts

## Employee claims

**Wrongful dismissal**: File within 1 year of dismissal notification with district court

**Wage claim**: Demand payment; if not paid within 2 weeks, file civil claim (5-year statute of limitations, but advisable to claim within 2-3 years)

**Discrimination claim**: File with district court or specialized equality body

**Burden of proof**: Generally shifts to employer to justify dismissal or differential treatment

## Available remedies

**Reinstatement**: Court can order return to same/similar position + back salary (minus other income earned)

**Compensation**: If reinstatement not feasible, damages including:

- Back pay (from dismissal to judgment, minus other income)
- Future lost earning capacity (typically 3-12 months)
- Moral damages (in egregious cases)
- Legal costs (usually shifted to losing party)

**Specific performance**: Court order to comply with contract (e.g., pay promised bonus)

**Injunctive relief**: Temporary order maintaining status quo pending trial (e.g., stay dismissal, continue salary)

## Administrative enforcement

**Labour Inspectorate (Inspectie SZW)**:

- Investigates complaints (anonymous possible)
- Issues fines for violations
- Can order compliance

**UWV oversight**:

- Monitors dismissal procedures
- Rejects unjustified dismissals
- Enforces long-term sickness and disability provisions

**Trade union/works council pressure**: Formal protests, legal action on behalf of members, collective resolution negotiations

## Alternative dispute resolution

**Mediation**: Voluntary; cost-effective; settlement is binding contract

**Arbitration**: Some CAOs provide binding arbitration (faster, often cheaper than court)

**Settlement agreements**: Parties agree to settle with negotiated severance/compensation; must be clearly documented
