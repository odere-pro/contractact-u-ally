# Termination & Dismissal

Procedures, grounds, and legal protections governing employment termination in the Netherlands.

## Key principle: Prohibition on unilateral termination

**Fundamental rule**: Employer cannot unilaterally terminate employment contract. Valid termination requires:

1. Written consent from employee, OR
2. UWV approval (for specific grounds), OR
3. Court order (for virtually any "urgent" reason)

This is one of Europe's strongest employment protections.

## Valid grounds for dismissal

**UWV Approval Path**:

- Economic/business reasons (lack of work, redundancy, restructuring, client loss)
- Long-term illness/disability (after 2 years, with rehabilitation attempts)
- Incapacity/lack of skills

**Court Order Path**:

- Gross misconduct (theft, violence, serious insubordination)
- Poor performance (if documented, improvement efforts made)
- Personality conflicts
- Confidentiality/non-compete breaches
- Habitual absenteeism/tardiness

**Never valid grounds**:

- Pregnancy or maternity status
- Disability
- Union membership/activity
- Taking statutory leave
- Reporting legal violations (whistleblowing)
- Age, gender, race, religion, sexual orientation

## Critical procedures

- **UWV route**: 4-8 week decision period; employer must prove justification; conditional approval possible
- **Court route**: 3-6 month process; judge assesses if dismissal is "urgent" and reasonable
- **Notice period**: Minimum 4 weeks to end of calendar month (unless probation or summary dismissal)
- **Severance**: 1/3 monthly salary × years worked (statutory; court can award more)
