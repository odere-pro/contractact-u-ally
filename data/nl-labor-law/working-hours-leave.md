# Working Hours & Leave

Statutory limits on work hours, rest requirements, and entitlement to paid and unpaid leave.

## Working hour limits

**Daily**: Maximum 12 hours per day (exceptions for emergencies rare and temporary)

**Weekly** (averaged):

- 55 hours over 4-week period
- 48 hours over 16-week period (legal baseline)

**Rest requirements**:

- 11 consecutive hours minimum between working days
- 35 consecutive hours weekly rest (can be split)
- Breaks: 15 min for 5-6 hr shifts; 30 min for 6+ hr shifts

**Night work**: Workers regularly working 10 PM–6 AM limited to 8 hours per 24-hour period

## Holiday entitlement

**Statutory minimum**: 4 × weekly working days

- Example: 5-day week = 20 days annual leave
- Example: 3-day week = 12 days annual leave

**Holiday allowance (Vakantiegeld)**: 8% minimum of gross annual salary (many CAOs offer more: 8.33%, 8.5%, 10%)

**Carryover**: Can be limited by contract (e.g., max 5 days to next year); at termination, unused holidays must be paid out

## Statutory leave types

| Type                                   | Duration                          | Payment               | Protection                          |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Annual leave (Betaald verlof)          | 4× work days/week                 | 100% + 8% allowance   | Portable; can be forced by employer |
| Maternity (Moederschap)                | 16 weeks                          | 100% (UWV reimburses) | Absolute protection; dismissal void |
| Paternity (Vaderschapsverlof)          | 2 days initial + 5 weeks optional | 100% initial; 70% UWV | Job protected                       |
| Parental (Ouderschapsverlof)           | 26 weeks per child per parent     | Negotiated/unpaid     | Job protected                       |
| Sickness (Ziekeverlof) — first 2 years | Continuous                        | Minimum 70%           | Employer pays                       |
| Public holidays                        | 3-5 statutory                     | 100% (as if worked)   | Fixed dates                         |

## Special rules

- **Holiday during sickness**: If sick during forced holiday, can claim as sick leave (requires medical cert)
- **Sick leave protection**: Cannot dismiss during first 2 years of continuous sickness; after 2 years requires UWV approval
- **Pregnancy protection**: Cannot force holiday during maternity leave period
