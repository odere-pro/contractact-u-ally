# Wages & Compensation

Statutory minimum wages, allowances, deductions, and equal pay principles.

## Minimum wage (as of January 2026)

Age-indexed hourly rates:

- **21+ years**: €14.40/hr
- **20 years**: €11.58/hr
- **19 years**: €9.21/hr
- **18 years**: €7.33/hr
- **Under 18**: €5.68/hr
- **Apprentices/Trainees**: ~32% of adult rate

**Automatic annual indexing** for inflation; applies to new pay cycles (mid-month application varies).

**Calculation**: Gross (before tax); includes base + regular allowances + shift premiums; excludes bonuses, one-time payments, and discretionary commissions (unless guaranteed).

## Holiday allowance (Vakantiegeld)

**Statutory**: 8% of gross annual earnings

- Based on all earnings (salary + bonuses + commission + overtime)
- Prorated for partial-year employment
- Can be paid lump-sum (May/June) or incorporated into monthly salary

**CAO overrides**: Many collective agreements specify 8.33%, 8.5%, 10%, etc. (binding if higher than 8%)

## Payment and payslips

**Frequency**: Monthly minimum; usually fixed date (e.g., 25th)

**Itemized payslip required**:

- Gross salary
- Deductions (tax, social security, etc.)
- Net salary
- Holiday pay accrued/paid
- Hours/overtime worked (if hourly)
- Reason for any deductions

**Prohibited practices**:

- Withholding pay as punishment
- Unilateral salary reduction without consent
- Deductions for regular wear and tear, breakage charges, administrative fees

## Equal pay

**Core principle**: Same pay for same job (gender, race, nationality, age, sexual orientation, disability, parental status all prohibited as basis for pay differentiation)
